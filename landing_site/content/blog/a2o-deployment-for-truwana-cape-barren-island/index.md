[Liz Znidersic](https://www.csu.edu.au/research/ilws/team/ilws-postgraduates/liz-znidersic) and [Professor Dave Watson](https://www.csu.edu.au/research/ilws/team/profiles/members/david-watson) recently deployed four acoustic sensors on truwana (Cape Barren Island) in Bass Strait, between Tasmania and the Australian mainland. Thank you to the Traditional Owners of truwana for supporting the A2O project.
