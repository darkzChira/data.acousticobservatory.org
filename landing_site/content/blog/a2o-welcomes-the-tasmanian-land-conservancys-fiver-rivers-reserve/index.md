Thank you to Dr Sally Bryant from the [Tasmanian Land Conservancy](https://tasland.org.au/) for deploying the A2O’s most southerly acoustic sensor set to date. Four sensors were recently established in the beautiful [Five Rivers Reserve](/sites/five-rivers-reserve-70/) in Tasmania’s Central Highlands, recording soundscapes among Snow Gums and along the banks of the Nive River.
