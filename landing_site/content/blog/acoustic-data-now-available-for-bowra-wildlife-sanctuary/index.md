Acoustic data from the [Australian Wildlife Conservancy’s Bowra Sanctuary](https://www.australianwildlife.org/where-we-work/bowra/) is now available! Sensors have been collecting information across Bowra for the past couple of months, recording many of the property’s arid bird species. Soundscapes range from dry Mulga plains ([Bowra Dry A](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/257) and [Bowra Dry B](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/259)) to the creeks and waterholes dotted throughout this unique landscape ([Bowra Wet A](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/258) and [Bowra Wet B](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/260)).
