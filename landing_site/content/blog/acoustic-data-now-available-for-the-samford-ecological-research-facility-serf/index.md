Acoustic data may now be accessed for the [Samford Ecological Research Facility (SERF)](http://www.serf.qut.edu.au/). SERF is managed by the [Queensland University of Technology](https://www.qut.edu.au/), and affiliated with the [Terrestrial Ecosystem Research Network (TERN)](https://supersites.tern.org.au/). These are the first available recordings for the A2O, with acoustic sensors actively monitoring the soundscapes of eucalypt woodland ([SERF Dry A](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/253) and [SERF Dry B](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/255)) and notophyll vine forest ([SERF Wet A](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/254) and [SERF Wet B](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/projects/1/sites/256)) on the outskirts of Brisbane in South-East Queensland.
