The A2O, in partnership with ABC Science and the University of New England, have developed Hoot Detective for National Science Week 2021. Hoot Detective is a digital citizen science project where participants can identify owl species, as well as other audible organisms, from across Australia. Anna Salleh’s article [Listening to the planet: Understanding the science of ecoacoustics](https://www.abc.net.au/news/science/2021-08-04/listening-to-the-planet-the-science-of-ecoacoustics/100325924) provides more information about the project. To start finding owls and other critters that call in the night, follow the link to [Hoot Detective](https://hootdetective.net.au/) now, and happy listening!
