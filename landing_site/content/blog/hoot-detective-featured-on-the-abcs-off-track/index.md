Hoot Detective, the citizen science project developed by ABC Science, the A2O and the University of New England, has been featured on Off Track (14/08/2021). Off Track is presented by Ann Jones and Jo Khan, and is an amazing radio program on the ABC about the sounds of nature, ecology, and the researchers behind the science. Please check out the program [here](https://www.abc.net.au/radionational/programs/offtrack/hunting-for-hoots-owl-ecoacoustics/13492922), featuring QUT’s very own Callan Alexander and Marina Scarpelli.
