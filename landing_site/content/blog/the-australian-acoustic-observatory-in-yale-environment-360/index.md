[‘Listening to Nature: The Emerging Field of Bioacoustics’](https://e360.yale.edu/features/listening-to-nature-the-emerging-field-of-bioacoustics) is a recent story written by [Adam Welz](https://e360.yale.edu/authors/adam-welz) for [Yale Environment 360](https://e360.yale.edu/about) exploring the growing use of acoustic technologies in ecological research. The Australian Acoustic Observatory gets a special mention, as does [Professor Dave Watson](https://www.csu.edu.au/research/ilws/team/profiles/members/david-watson), one the observatory’s Chief Investigator Managers.
