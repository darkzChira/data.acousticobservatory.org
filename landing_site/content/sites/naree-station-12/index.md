summary: "Located on the inland floodplains of northern NSW, Naree sits at the head of the nationally significant wetlands of the Cuttaburra Channels and Yantabulla Swamp. The site is composed of alluvial swamps with scattered Coolabah trees, and riparian areas of Coolabah, Black-box and mixed shrub communities (Eremophila, Senna and Dodonaea species). Mulga woodlands and sandplain vegetation communities are also found in the area."
